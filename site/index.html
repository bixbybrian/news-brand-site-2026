Four Directions

NEWS doesn't stand for headlines. NEWS is North, East, West, South: four directions around one fixed point, suggesting movement and a wider view. Everything we produce carries that orientation.

Shop

The NEWS Shop isn't just a storefront. It is a physical anchor in Santa Fe for original artwork, printed matter, and house goods: a gathering point for studio and archive under one fixed roof.

Made With Intent

NEWS makes things with a point of view. Thoughtful pieces, images, words, and ideas that ask us to look a little closer at the world we share, and at the changes shaping it.

Founded

NEWS was founded by artist and designer Sam Mott as a clothing project built around a clear point of view: clothing as a medium, direction as a philosophy. That orientation, fixed to a single point on the map, still guides what NEWS makes today.
