The Name:
Four Directions

NEWS doesn't stand for headlines. It stands for North, East, West, South, four compass points that together suggest direction, movement, and a wider view. Every garment carries that orientation.

The Mission:
Made With Intent

NEWS uses clothing as a medium. The graphics, images, and text printed on each shirt are chosen to speak directly to climate change: its effects on people, animals, and the landscapes we share. The brand is not a fundraising vehicle. It is a point of view, made wearable.

About the Shirts

Each NEWS tee is cut and sewn in Peru from ring-spun cotton, then screenprinted by hand. Inside the neck, a set of GPS coordinates points to a remote stretch of the California coast, a major biogeographic boundary known for its stark landscape and natural boundaries. A fixed point. Repeated, quietly, on every shirt.

Prints are produced in limited runs. Each image is chosen for what it says, not only how it looks. When a run is gone, it's gone. No restocks.

Founded

NEWS was founded by artist and designer Sam Mott as a clothing project built around a clear point of view. Its name, drawn from North, East, West, and South, reflects a belief in direction, both visual and philosophical. From the beginning, the intention was to make clothing that felt considered, purposeful, and connected to something larger than trend. That foundation continues to guide the brand today.
